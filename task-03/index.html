Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about membership options, billing flexibility, and building facilities.

All memberships include multi-gigabit symmetrical fiber Wi-Fi, ergonomic standing desks and seating, phone booth access, specialty espresso & kombucha bar, kitchen privileges, standard mailing address services, and invite access to weekly tech founder talks and demo nights.

Hot desk and dedicated desk plans are 100% month-to-month with a simple 30-day notice policy. There are no lock-in setup fees or termination penalties. Annual plans offer a 20% discount with payment upfront and can be transferred to any of our 18 global locations seamlessly.

Our FabLab features Formlabs 3+ SLA printers, Bambu Lab X1-Carbon multi-color FDM printers, Epilog laser cutters, Hakko temperature-controlled soldering stations with HEPA fume extraction, Rigol 4-channel oscilloscopes, thermal imaging cameras, and basic hand prototyping tools.

Dedicated desk and private studio members enjoy full 24/7/365 biometric access through encrypted mobile NFC credentials. Hot desk members have standard access from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM Monday through Saturday, with optional 24/7 keycard upgrades available anytime.

You can easily add or remove team members via your digital member dashboard. Team billing scales proportionally, and teams of 5 or more automatically receive unified monthly invoicing, pooled conference room credits, and dedicated Slack channels with our community management staff.
